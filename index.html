About

Who is iMKRXSHER?

Hendra Kurniawan

Freelance graphic designer based in Pare, Kediri — East Java. Operating under the brand iMKRXSHER, I specialize in logo redraw and vector recreation, turning low-resolution or pixelated logos into clean, print-ready files.

Every project is approached with attention to detail — whether it's a complex shape built from scratch with Pen Tool, or integrating downloaded assets into a cohesive composition.
